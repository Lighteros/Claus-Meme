How it works

Move with A/D and jump with Space. Hold E or click and hold a ledge to grab it. Release before grabbing again. On a phone, use the on-screen buttons.

Red Clauses wind up before throwing. Dodge their shots to avoid a shove.

Climb as high as you can. Touch water and your run ends. There is no time limit. The water gradually gets faster, up to a steady maximum.

Your best climb earns your score. The Top 10 keeps each player’s best run; survival time breaks a tie. Play alone, without a wallet.

Trades in the main $CLAUS pool add small waves once included on Robinhood. Waves are limited to at most one every 12 seconds. Water rises between trades too.
