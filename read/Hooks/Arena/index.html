Rising Tide

A free singleplayer climbing game. Enter a username and start immediately; no wallet or tokens are needed.

A trade in the main $CLAUS pool can create a small extra wave in each running game once included on Robinhood. The hook records at most one pulse every 12 seconds; each run accepts at most eight within any two-minute window. Normal water rises between trades too. Network interruptions can delay or skip waves.

The game and leaderboard run on a server. The hook supplies market pulses; trading fees and their allocation are unchanged. There are no cash or token prizes.

Move with A/D or arrow keys, jump with Space, and hold E or a ledge to grab. Touching water ends your run. Climb as high as you can, with no summit or time limit. Water gradually gets faster up to a steady maximum. The Top 10 ranks each player’s best height; survival time breaks a tie. Retry starts immediately.
