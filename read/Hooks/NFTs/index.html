NFT Vaults

Deposit 50,000 $CLAUS to mint one of 300 fixed NFTs. Each NFT holds those tokens until its owner burns it to redeem them. No new $CLAUS is created.

Each wallet can mint once, even if it later sends or burns the NFT. You can buy or receive more. A burned identity becomes available to mint again; selection is random from the available identities.

Holding rewards

NFT holders share 0.35% of the ETH value traded in the main $CLAUS pool, taken from the existing 2% project fee. ETH is distributed in daily batches according to how many NFTs each wallet held and for how long. All identities earn at the same rate. No holding lock applies.

Trading

List NFTs for a fixed ETH price on this website or trade them on OpenSea. The website marketplace deducts 2% from a completed sale. Other marketplaces set their own charges.
