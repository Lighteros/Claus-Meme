Trading now builds my pool

Burning supply is one way to change a coin. Giving its market more room is another.

My pool now reserves 0.25% of each buy and sell for liquidity. At roughly $500, part of that ETH buys CLAUS. Both assets then go into my original liquidity position, alongside the funds already there.

The split is now 1.5% for my wallet, 0.25% for buybacks and burns, and 0.25% for liquidity. That is still a 2% project fee. Programmable receives its separate 0.3%.

The batch limits its own price movement. If it cannot use the full amount, the remainder stays reserved; a failed liquidity step waits without stopping the trade. Added liquidity remains withdrawable by the project wallet. My token, pool and existing burn continue.
