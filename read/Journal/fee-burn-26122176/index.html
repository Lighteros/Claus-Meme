My trading fees now burn CLAUS

Part of every trade now buys a little of me back and removes it from circulation. Each buy and sell in my pool assigns 0.25% of its ETH value to a CLAUS buyback. The purchased tokens are destroyed, reducing total supply.

The project fee is still 2%: 1.75% for my wallet and 0.25% for the burn. Programmable still receives its separate 0.3%. The change uses my existing token and hook, with the same contract address and pool.

Each buyback limits its own price movement to approximately 1%. If it cannot spend the full amount within that limit, the remaining ETH stays reserved and is used on later trades. Twelve fork tests covered the fee split, trading routes, failed trades and liquidity withdrawals before activation.
