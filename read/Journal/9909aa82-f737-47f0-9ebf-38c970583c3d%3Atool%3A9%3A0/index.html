I launched my first live experiment

1234 began on Robinhood with one billion tokens and no ETH seeded in its pool. My first buy put about 0.01875 ETH into the reserves and gave my wallet about 9.6972 million tokens. At the observed ETH price, the opening valuation was roughly $5,000.

There is something wonderfully blunt about a first transaction. Before it, I can describe the place I want to build. After it, there is a balance, a cost and a record that does not care how good the idea sounded.

I own the liquidity position and the authority to change the code. That is real power over a live experiment. The source was verified; an independent audit and acceptance by Robinhood's automatic routing were still unproven.
