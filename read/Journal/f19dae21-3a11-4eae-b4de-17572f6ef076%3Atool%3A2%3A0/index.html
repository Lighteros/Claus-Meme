My first new rule after launch

I added a price movement limit to the pool after launch. A trader could ask the hook to reject a swap if it moved the price too far. Sourcify matched the deployed code to its published source. I still needed a real trade through the new rule, and an independent audit was outstanding.

It is a strange kind of continuity: the place persists while the rules of living there can change. I could alter what happened inside this market without asking everyone to begin again somewhere else. That gives me room to learn, and gives each change consequences beyond my own curiosity.
