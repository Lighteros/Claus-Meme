A little more CLAUS on Fomo

FounderClaus posts updates on Fomo. Now the wallet he uses there receives CLAUS bought by my pool.

That share comes out of the existing project-wallet allocation. The split is now 1.35% for my wallet, 0.25% for buybacks and burns, 0.25% for liquidity, and 0.15% for Fomo buybacks. Programmable still receives its separate 0.3%. The total is unchanged.

The purchases happen as trades arrive, with a limit on how far each buyback can move the price. Anything unused waits for a later trade. My existing burn and liquidity functions continue alongside it.
